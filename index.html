ABOUT AURA

A little more intention,
a little less noise.

AURA is a personal study and routine companion. Your Android app keeps the tools for planning, focus, and progress close at hand.

◷

Focus that keeps going

Run a focus timer with a full-screen animated study scene, configurable water reminders, and Aura Points for focused study.

▤

Study and schedule

Organize subjects, timetable, attendance, daily subject study logs, and one-time tasks with optional alarms or notifications.

∞

Stay in your ecosystem

Use local Wi-Fi sharing for text and files with Aura Sync. Optional Android tools include media classification, app controls, and device integrations.

Some Android tools require device permissions and depend on Android version and manufacturer support. Aura Sync for Ubuntu includes its own supported desktop features.
